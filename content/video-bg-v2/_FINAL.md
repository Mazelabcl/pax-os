## VIDEO 1 — Deep dive into Pax world

### Plain-English description (15 seconds, single continuous dolly)

Imagine the camera floating in a void of deep violet. Suddenly, filling the entire frame, a single crystal pulses — magenta-violet, translucent, alive, breathing slow on dark damp Pax rock. Tiny golden particles drift toward it as if pulled by the light it gives off. The camera, in no rush, begins to pull back. The crystal is not alone: it is embedded in a wall threaded with veins of violet light that move like roots. Smaller crystals appear in the periphery, each pulsing at its own rhythm — this is not an object, this is an ecosystem.

The camera keeps drifting back and down. Carved cornices appear, stone columns, an archway of crystals — Pax architecture, found and shaped at the same time. We pass through the arch into a massive open cavern, the kind of space that takes a breath out of you. Magenta dominates, but electric cyan beams slice down from above and a soft golden mist hugs the floor. Reflections shimmer on a mirror-still pool. A small green-jade Pax cyclops crosses a distant bridge holding a crystal, and the scale finally lands: this world is enormous, and the Pax are tiny inside it, living.

We descend further. A phosphorescent river of liquid light cuts through the lower levels. The camera reaches a vantage point and the full reveal hits: dozens of crystals connected by fine golden threads, a three-dimensional constellation floating in the air of the cavern. A golden pulse races through the constellation, crystal to crystal — the entire cave is one breathing organism. The camera reaches its final position. In extreme foreground, out of focus, another violet-magenta crystal frames the entire world behind it. We started in a crystal. We end in a crystal. The world was always there, waiting to be seen.

## VIDEO 2 — Gem macro to chase cam

### Plain-English description (15 seconds, multi-shot intentional)

Imagine the camera holding still on a single violet-magenta crystal — but this one is shaped like a small obelisk, cleaner than the wild crystal of video 1. It pulses on dark Pax stone, alone, golden particles suspended around it. Then it pulses harder, brighter — something is about to happen. The camera retreats slowly. We see the crystal sits in a small alcove of violet rock with bioluminescent moss and Pax runes carved into the wall. A shadow enters from the left of the frame: a small green-jade hand reaches in, fingers extended carefully toward the crystal. The hand closes around it, and the crystal flashes gold for half a beat in response.

Hard cut. Now we see Jiggy fully — the green-jade Pax cyclops, single huge turquoise eye wide with delight, violet bandana on his forehead, holding the crystal up close to his face with the biggest smile. The platform behind him glows magenta. For one beat his eye meets the camera — not a wink, not a wave, just a glance that invites you in. Then he turns and runs.

The camera latches onto his back from three-quarters above, drone-style. He sprints across a Pax stone bridge, crystal in fist, magenta and cyan lights streaking past him. Another invisible cut — the drone pulls wider, lower, almost skimming the floor of glittering crystal fragments. He enters a tunnel of pure aqua-cyan crystals. Light moves with him. Golden sparks fly at his every step. He explodes out of the tunnel onto an elevated platform — and behind him, in full reveal, the Pax cavern world opens in coral explosion: magenta and cyan and gold all at once. He runs across a thin bridge over a phosphorescent river. The camera orbits him. The crystal in his hand glows brighter and brighter. He crouches into a wind-up. The camera drops to ground level in front of him. He leaps — and freezes mid-air, arms wide, crystal blazing in his hand, the entire Pax world glowing behind him in its absolute maximum brightness. A single beat of joy frozen forever.

## Production notes (for Aldot, when uploading to Seedance platform)

### Recommended tier (May 2026 research)
Seedance 2 **Pro** — not Lite. The complex camera moves (continuous dolly + drone chase + invisible cuts) need Pro-tier quality. Approx cost: $0.20-0.40 per second × 15s × 2 videos × 2 ratios = $24-48 USD total Seedance bill.

### Generation strategy
1. **Generate Video 1 first** (single-shot dolly is simpler — validates style + palette canon).
2. If Video 1 looks Pax-correct, generate Video 2 in single-shot Plan A.
3. If Video 2 multi-shot fails continuity → fallback to Plan B (3 × 5s clips, dissolve in post 0.2s).
4. Generate 16:9 first (primary desktop hero). 9:16 second after 16:9 validates.

### Seamless loop note
Both videos end on visual elements close to their opening frames (V1: extreme foreground crystal; V2: hero leap with bright crystal). In post, add a 0.5s crossfade from final frame to initial frame to enable seamless infinite loop on the website.

### What to do if a generation drifts off-canon
- If crystals come out blue/cyan instead of violet-magenta → reinforce palette hex codes in prompt, regenerate.
- If Jiggy shows two eyes → add "ONE central eye ONLY, never two eyes" three times consecutively in the prompt body.
- If aesthetic drifts to photorealistic → emphasize "stylized painterly 3D animation, NOT photorealistic".

### Loops without sound (web autoplay)
Both prompts include native audio generation for **future reuse** (Reels/TikTok cuts). The website will autoplay-muted. Native audio is zero extra cost on Seedance and doubles the asset value.
