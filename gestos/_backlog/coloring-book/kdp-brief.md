## Descripcion Amazon (max 4000 chars)

Discover the magical underground world of the Pax Tribe! Color your way through crystal caverns, meet playful cyclops alchemists, and explore a universe powered by human kindness.

This coloring book features 40+ hand-crafted pages with thick, bold outlines perfect for all ages — from young adventurers learning their first strokes to adults seeking mindful relaxation.

Inside you will find:
- Individual character portraits of Jiggy, Wiz, KZ, Fortis, and the whole Pax clan
- Epic scenes from the Forgotten Temple and Crystal Gardens of Uray Pacha
- Heartwarming real-world scenes where acts of kindness charge underground crystals
- A full map of the Uray Pacha underground world
- Clean, thick outlines — no tiny details, no frustration

Every page connects to the Pax philosophy: every act of kindness matters. Every gesture counts. Color the world, one crystal at a time.
